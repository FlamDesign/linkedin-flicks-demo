# Flicks on LinkedIn: concept demo

Clickable concept showing Flicks across LinkedIn's ad formats, seen by a fictional member (Jordan Ellis):

- Feed, desktop: vertical video ad (Acer), Thought Leader ad (Cross), square video ad (American Express), in-stream pre-roll on a creator video (Acer), Spotlight ad in the right rail (American Express), and a Conversation ad in the messaging window (Acer)
- Messaging: Message ad with a companion banner (Cross), Conversation ad with a Lead Gen Form that pre-fills the model the member last viewed (Acer)
- Mobile app: square video ad, vertical video ad and Thought Leader ad

Open "Ad formats" at the bottom right to jump to each one. All people, companies other than the advertisers, posts, counts and the member profile are invented. Built by Flam for partner conversations. Not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn, Acer, American Express or Cross.

Open `index.html` in Chrome, or visit the GitHub Pages URL for this repo.
