# Flicks on LinkedIn: concept demo

Clickable concept showing Flicks in each LinkedIn ad format, seen by a fictional member (Jordan Ellis). The layout is responsive: LinkedIn's three-column desktop feed narrows to its mobile web layout on phones.

Formats: Video ad (American Express), Conversation ad with Lead Gen Form (Acer), In-stream video ad on a creator video (Acer), Single image ad (Airbnb), Thought Leader ad (Cross), Spotlight ad (American Express, wide screens), Message ad with companion banner (Cross, wide screens).

Open "Ad formats" at the bottom right to jump to each one. All people, companies other than the advertisers, posts and counts are invented. Profile photos are AI-generated faces (this-person-does-not-exist dataset on Hugging Face, MIT licence) so no real person is shown. Scene photos are from Unsplash. Built by Flam for partner conversations. Not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn, Acer, American Express, Airbnb or Cross.
